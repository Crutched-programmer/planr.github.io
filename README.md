# Welcome to Planr - Your Personal AI Study Planner

Planr is a modern, AI-powered web application designed to help students organize their study schedules and enhance their learning experience. Built with Next.js, Genkit, and a sleek, translucent "liquid glass" UI, Planr provides a suite of intelligent tools to create personalized and effective study routines. The application is entirely client-side, ensuring all your personal data is stored locally in your browser for complete privacy.

## Key Features

### 1. Personalized Profile
Set up your unique academic profile, including your class, curriculum, school schedule, and even your hobbies. This profile serves as the foundation for the AI to generate plans that are perfectly tailored to your life.

### 2. AI-Powered Daily Study Plan
Enter your homework, deadlines, and other commitments for the day, and let the AI generate a detailed, minute-by-minute study schedule. The plan intelligently balances study time, breaks, and personal activities, and you can even provide feedback to the AI to refine and improve the plan on the spot.

### 3. Interactive Quiz Generator
Test your knowledge and reinforce your learning by generating custom multiple-choice quizzes on any topic. The AI creates questions of varying difficulty, complete with timers, scoring, and explanations for each answer, helping you prepare for exams and master your subjects.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js & React
- **AI/Generative:** Google's Genkit
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with ShadCN UI components
- **Animation:** OGL (WebGL library) for the background particle effect.
